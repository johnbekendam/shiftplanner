title: Schedule is now Demand
audience: admin
---
The **Schedule** page is now called **Demand**. It shows one workcenter at a time:

- **Default demand**: add or remove shifts, and set the number of slots for each day of the week.
- **Calendar**: green days are fully staffed, yellow days have open slots. A day with its own slot count has a border.
- **Date card**: click a day to change its slots for that date only. "Reset to default" goes back to the weekday values.

You change the slots of one date on the Demand page now, not on the Planning page. Your changes are saved when you click **Save**.
