date: 2026-10-03
title: Dashboard shows the demand
audience: admin, manager
---
The dashboard chart now has three lines:

- **Demand**: the slots of all workcenters per week. This line shows on the **Overall** card only. It is off when you open the dashboard. Select the **Demand** checkbox to show it.
- **Available**: the hours of the confirmed employees. This line was called **Confirmed**.
- **Planned**: the planned hours per week.

When **Planned** reaches **Demand**, all slots of that week are filled. The **Unconfirmed** and **Total** lines are gone. The dashed target line stays.

Each card now has two donuts, in the color of their line:

- **Available**: the available hours as a percentage of the target.
- **Planned**: the planned hours as a percentage of the available hours.

A donut shows only when its line is on.
